# Adventure of Sea Animal

A gentle underwater game for young children.

## Why I created this game

I'm not a game developer. I'm a father, and I made this game with all my heart, hoping to see my son's speech improve while he plays. I had support from Claude AI and my friends.

This game may not be perfect, and others may not find it fun. But seeing my son smile and feel happy makes me the happiest person on earth.

I hope this game will help other parents too.

## What children can do

- Find sea friends and hear their names
- Count from 1 to 10 by swimming through rings
- Collect stars and open treasure boxes
- Play without enemies or time limits
